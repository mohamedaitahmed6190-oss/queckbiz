Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

At QueckBiz, your privacy is not just a policy — it is built into how our tools work. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect (and do not collect), how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. Please read it carefully.

1. Our Core Privacy Principle

QueckBiz tools run entirely in your web browser. All document data you enter — client names, invoice amounts, business details, addresses, and any other information — is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. We do not store your business documents, and we have no access to the content of any document you create using our tools.

This is not a marketing claim — it is an architectural fact. Our tools are static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files. There is no backend server receiving your form data, no database storing your invoices, and no account system holding your information. When you close a QueckBiz tool, the data you entered disappears.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

The following categories of information are never collected, stored, or transmitted by QueckBiz:

  • Names, addresses, or contact details you enter into our document tools
  • Invoice amounts, payment terms, or financial figures
  • Client or customer information entered into any form field
  • Documents you create, download, or print using our tools
  • Business registration numbers, tax IDs, or banking details
  • Passwords or authentication credentials (we have no accounts system)

3. Information We Do Collect

Like virtually all websites, QueckBiz collects a limited amount of non-personally identifying technical data to understand how the site is used and to serve advertising. This includes:

3.1 Analytics Data (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate traffic patterns on QueckBiz. This service may collect your approximate geographic location (country and region level), browser type and version, device type, pages visited, and how long you spent on each page. This data is aggregated and anonymized — we cannot identify you from this information. Google Analytics data is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

If you decline cookies when prompted by our consent banner, analytics tracking will not be activated. You can also opt out of Google Analytics at any time using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3.2 Advertising Data (Google AdSense)

QueckBiz displays advertisements through Google AdSense to fund the platform's operation. Google AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your interests. If you accept cookies, AdSense may collect information about your browsing behavior to personalize the ads you see. If you decline cookies, non-personalized ads will be displayed instead.

You can manage your Google advertising preferences at adssettings.google.com, or visit our Ad Settings page for more options.

3.3 Local Storage

QueckBiz uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) to save your theme preference (light or dark mode) and your language preference. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

4. How We Use Collected Information

The limited data we collect through analytics and advertising is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Understanding which tools and pages are most used so we can prioritize improvements
  • Detecting and troubleshooting technical issues
  • Displaying advertisements that help fund the free operation of QueckBiz
  • Complying with legal obligations

We do not sell your data to any third party. We do not use your data for direct marketing. We do not share your data with any party other than our analytics and advertising partners (Google) as described above.

5. Cookies and Consent

We use a consent banner on first visit to ask for your permission before activating analytics tracking and personalized advertising cookies. If you accept, both are activated. If you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown and analytics are not tracked. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or visiting our Cookie Policy page.

6. Third-Party Services

QueckBiz uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics 4 — Website traffic analytics. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — Display advertising. Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Pages — Website hosting and content delivery. Cloudflare Privacy Policy

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. Because QueckBiz does not collect or store personal information beyond standard server logs (managed by Cloudflare), most data rights requests are not applicable to us. However:

  • You can opt out of Google Analytics at any time using the browser add-on linked above.
  • You can manage Google advertising preferences at adssettings.google.com.
  • You can withdraw cookie consent at any time by clearing your browser's local storage.
  • EU/EEA users have rights under GDPR; UK users under UK GDPR; California users under CCPA.

For any privacy-related requests or questions, contact us at support@queckbiz.com.

8. Data Security

Because QueckBiz does not collect or store your document data, there is no database of your business information to protect. The minimal technical data collected by Google Analytics and AdSense is subject to Google's enterprise-grade security infrastructure. Our website is served exclusively over HTTPS, and we implement standard security headers to prevent common web vulnerabilities.

9. Children's Privacy

QueckBiz is designed for use by adults operating businesses. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has used our service in a way that has resulted in data collection, please contact us immediately at support@queckbiz.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of QueckBiz after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at support@queckbiz.com. We will respond within 48 hours on business days.

12. GDPR Compliance (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or UK GDPR applies to your use of QueckBiz. Under these regulations, you have the following rights regarding any personal data we process:

  • Right of access — You have the right to request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to erasure — You have the right to request deletion of your personal data, where we have a legal basis to hold it.
  • Right to restrict processing — You have the right to request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability — You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — You have the right to object to processing of your personal data for direct marketing or legitimate interest purposes.

Because QueckBiz does not collect or store document data, most of these rights apply only to the minimal technical data collected through Google Analytics (which you can opt out of using the Google Analytics opt-out tool). To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@queckbiz.com.

13. CCPA Compliance (California Users)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives you specific rights regarding your personal information. You have the right to know what personal information is collected about you, the right to delete personal information we have collected, the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising your CCPA rights.

QueckBiz does not sell personal information to any third party. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. The only personal data-adjacent information we process is anonymized analytics data through Google Analytics, from which you can opt out at any time.

14. International Data Transfers

QueckBiz is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which operates a global content delivery network. Your visit to QueckBiz may involve your data being processed in countries outside your own, including the United States. Google Analytics and Google AdSense also process data in the United States. These transfers are conducted under appropriate legal mechanisms including Google's Standard Contractual Clauses for international data transfers. By using QueckBiz, you consent to these transfers in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

15. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that sends a signal to websites requesting that your browsing activity not be tracked. QueckBiz respects your privacy through our consent-based approach: if you decline cookies when prompted, analytics tracking is not activated regardless of your browser's DNT setting. If you accept cookies, analytics may be active. We recommend using the consent banner as your primary privacy control on QueckBiz rather than relying solely on browser-level DNT signals.
