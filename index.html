About QueckBiz

QueckBiz is a free suite of business document generators built for freelancers, contractors, and small business owners. Instead of paying for accounting software or fighting with spreadsheet templates, you can create an invoice, receipt, estimate, proforma invoice, credit note, or purchase order in your browser in under a minute — then download it as a PDF or print it directly. Every tool runs entirely on your device: nothing you type is uploaded to a server, so your client names, prices, and business details stay private.

Because there is no account to set up and nothing to install, QueckBiz suits anyone who bills clients only occasionally and does not want the overhead of full accounting software. Each document is designed to look professional out of the box, with room for your business name, contact details, and — where relevant — your logo, so the paperwork you send reflects your brand. And because everything is generated on your own device, it keeps working even without an internet connection.

How QueckBiz works

  1. Pick the document you need — invoice, receipt, estimate, proforma, credit note, or purchase order.
  2. Fill in your business and client details, then add your line items, tax, and any discount.
  3. Download the finished document as a PDF or print it directly — no signup, no watermark.
